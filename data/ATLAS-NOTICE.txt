Generated assets retain their upstream licenses. They are not relicensed under

hash, source map paths and an inventory of referenced icon files. Attribution
and source links remain available in the viewer. The upstream commit history
provides individual authorship details. Consult data/UPSTREAM-README.md and
data/asset-sources.json in a built site.
